# Family Schedule

A private schedule app that runs on your phone. Your data stays on the phone. GitHub only hosts the app's code.

## Put it online (once, ~15 min, easiest on a computer)

1. Unzip `family-schedule.zip`.
2. Go to github.com and create a free account.
3. Click **+** (top right), then **New repository**. Name it `family-schedule`, keep it **Public**, and click **Create repository**.
4. On the new page, click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in everything from the unzipped folder, including the `icons` folder. Click **Commit changes**.
5. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Branch," pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
6. Wait 1–2 minutes. Your address appears at the top of that page, for example `https://yourname.github.io/family-schedule/`.

## Install on your Android phone

1. Open that address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮ → Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open it from the new icon. It works offline from then on.

## Proton events

1. On the Proton **web** app, go to **Settings → Calendars**. Pick your shared calendar, then go to **Share with anyone → Create link → Full view**. Copy the link.
2. In the app, go to **More → Proton calendar**. Paste the link and tap **Save link**.
3. To refresh, tap **Open link**. Chrome downloads a file. Open the download, tap **Share**, and choose **Family Schedule**. You can also use **Choose .ics file**.
4. Tap any Proton event to say who it's for. The app remembers by title.

## Backups

**More → Backup & restore → Back up now.** Save the file to Proton Drive. The app reminds you after 7 days.
Uninstalling the app or clearing Chrome's data for the site erases everything that wasn't backed up.

## Updating the app

Upload the new files to the same GitHub repository, replacing the old ones. The next time you open the app, close and reopen it once to load the new version. Your data isn't touched.
